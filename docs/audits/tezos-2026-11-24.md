Ed25519 key, XTZ balance and history, and XTZ transfers forged and signed in
core. The readings below were taken on 2026-11-24 (UTC) without API keys;
every network request was a read (GETs and `simulate_operation`).

## Operation encoding

From the protocol's own source, `src/proto_025_PsUshuai/lib_protocol/operation_repr.ml`
(octez master, read 2026-11-24):

- A manager operation is its tag, then `source` (a public key hash: a scheme
  tag and 20 bytes), `fee`, `counter`, `gas_limit` and `storage_limit` as
  Zarith naturals. `reveal` is tag 107 (`0x6b`) and carries
  `public_key` (scheme tag and key) and an optional `proof` (one presence
  byte; only BLS keys carry one). `transaction` is tag 108 (`0x6c`) and
  carries `amount`, `destination` (22 bytes: `00` and a key hash, or `01`, a
  contract hash and a padding byte) and optional `parameters` (absent for
  `Unit` at the default entrypoint).
- An Ed25519, secp256k1 or P-256 signature is the operation's last 64 bytes,
  untagged; BLS and ML-DSA-44 signatures carry a prefix within the contents.

Taquito 25.1.1's `@taquito/local-forging` forges for `PsUshuai9…` and its
reveal schema has the `proof` field. Forging three operations included on
mainnet with it and appending the signature each carries reproduces each
operation's hash (`ootbVsgx…` at level 15,953,142, a reveal and transfer;
`ooUdbpEt…` and `oo4tJTxK…` at 15,953,458, a tz1 and a tz2 transfer), and the
Ed25519 signature of the first verifies over BLAKE2b-256 of `03` and the
forged bytes. `core/tests/fixtures/tezos-vectors.json` holds them.

## Fees, gas and storage

The default mempool filter, `src/proto_025_PsUshuai/lib_plugin/mempool.ml`:
a batch's fees must reach `minimal_fees` (100 mutez) plus
`minimal_nanotez_per_gas_unit` (100) for each unit of its gas limits plus
`minimal_nanotez_per_byte` (1,000) for each byte of the operation, shell
header (the 32-byte branch) and signature included.

`simulate_operation` on the nodes below:

| Operation | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 mutez from a revealed tz1 to an empty tz1 | applied; `consumed_milligas` 2,168,788; `allocated_destination_contract` true; 64,250 mutez burned (257 × 250) |
| 1 mutez to a funded tz1, and to itself | applied; 2,168,788 milligas, nothing burned |
| More than the balance | `failed`, `contract.balance_too_low` |
| Unit to a contract whose parameter is not `unit` | `failed`, `michelson_v1.bad_contract_parameter` |
| A transfer from an unrevealed account | HTTP 500, `contract.unrevealed_key` |
| A reveal naming another account's key | HTTP 500, `contract.manager.inconsistent_hash` |

The lone transfer's 2,168.788 gas includes the batch's signature check; the
same transfer behind a reveal used 2,101 (TzKT `gasUsed` of `ootbVsgx…`),
and a tz1 reveal 171 (TzKT's latest reveals; Taquito's `REVEAL_GAS_LIMIT.TZ1`
is also 171). `run_operation` answered HTTP 401 on rpc.tzbeta.net, so
simulations use `simulate_operation`. Taquito branches from `head~2`
(`prepare-provider.ts`), a block Tenderbake has made final.
